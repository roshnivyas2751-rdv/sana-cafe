The Sana Story

Born from
South India.

Sana is our love letter to South Indian mornings — a place for golden dosas, strong coffee, homemade chutneys and unhurried conversations.

Made
with

A little taste
of home.

We grew up around kitchens where breakfast was never just breakfast. It was conversation, laughter, newspaper pages and the sound of a steel tumbler meeting a saucer.

Sana brings those memories into a modern café. Our recipes stay rooted in tradition while our space is designed for today's generation.
